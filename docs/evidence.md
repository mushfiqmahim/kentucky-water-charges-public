# Evidence and interpretation

This edition accompanies the working paper revised October 2, 2026. The paper and supplement give the full methods and district evidence. These notes explain the sample rules, consequential source decisions and limits relevant to using the data.

## Sample and measurement

The original matched-name frame contains 97 districts. Historical tariff review selected all 17 initial account decliners and 17 randomly drawn from 80 nondecliners, using seed 20260920. A separate Daviess merger correction reduces the frame to 96; Daviess was not in the tariff draw. Of 34 selected districts, 32 have reconstructed price pairs, 27 have supported pairs and 20 enter the primary analysis. The [complete disposition](../results/Selected_34_Disposition_v4.md) records every selected district.

The outcome is the approved pre-tax monthly water charge at December 31, 2019 and 2024 for a designated ordinary small retail connection using 4,000 gallons. It includes supported recurring water charges and excludes sewer, taxes and nonrecurring fees. The 2,000- and 6,000-gallon scenarios are sensitivity calculations, not additional independent districts. Reported residential accounts are PSC account 461.1, not population or occupied-household counts.

A supported pair requires a traceable ordinary retail category, separable geographic exceptions, supported component applicability and effective dates, and no documented category transition that makes the benchmark incomparable. District-wide counts are not generally allocated to that category: geographic and meter-size shares remain unknown.

The primary account screen requires all six annual observations, unchanged reported county sets, no documented in-window merger, no absolute annual end-account change above 10%, and no beginning/prior-end discrepancy above 1%. Passing these rules does not prove stable physical boundaries or perfect classifications. Earlier results were known when the decisions were recorded; the review was not blinded.

## Regulatory reconstruction

The current ledger contains 68 schedule/authorization records, eight amendment/clarification records and six recurring-charge records: 82 logical records with 43 distinct linked case IDs. Schedule records include 20 anchors and 48 subsequent entries; 45 subsequent entries change the benchmark arithmetic and three do not. One approval is superseded at the same effective date. These counts do not establish legally operative tariff periods, distinct implemented changes or complete billing spells.

All 20 base-price bridges reconcile to the endpoint differences. Missing increases followed by reversals could still leave that arithmetic intact. Document dates, service-effective dates, first invoices and collections remain separate. E057 is a wholesale special-contract amendment, not an ordinary retail review; [coding provenance](../data/coding_provenance.json) preserves the consequential classification correction.

The source notes and supplement retain these material qualifications:

- Knott's detailed retail increase of $677,818 and summary amount of $646,151 differ by $31,667. The detailed table reconciles internally, but the bridge to the summary remains unestablished. Neither figure determines the endpoint tariff.
- Mountain has conflicting two-/four-month refund wording and 48-month versus January 2024–December 2028 duration wording. Its 2026 order also gives $1,365,608 assessed on PDF p38 and $1,368,516 collected on p49, a $2,908 cap difference. No corrected cap or cessation date is imputed; the supported December 2024 $1.72 component and $56.63 benchmark remain unchanged.
- Hyden-Leslie's financial requirements are regulatory pro-forma amounts, not observed costs. The earlier phase-two invoice date remains unresolved. The cessation order supports excluding the obsolete $1.53 charge at December 2024; its last billed month was November 2024.
- Henderson's reporting initially copied billings into collections; these are not independently verified receipts. Western Fleming has evidence of a collection start, not a verified first invoice. Its monitoring filing is linked rather than bundled because banking pages contain account identifiers. Powell's first surcharge invoice and Estill's complete billing spell remain unknown.

## Source access

[access.csv](../sources/access.csv) lists all 213 expected originals: 54 bundled administrative orders, 158 originals available by reference and one held index with an unresolved original URL. Source bytes and checksum identities are retained; no redacted document is substituted under an original checksum. The collection is selective. Public availability is not a redistribution license, and utility-authored filings and institutional HTML are not bundled where redistribution remains unresolved; retained orders do not imply clearance of all PSC records. Original-host access, failed retrievals and unknown source URLs remain explicit in [unresolved_references.json](../sources/unresolved_references.json).
